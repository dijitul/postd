ManyChat is excellent at automated Instagram and Messenger campaigns: "comment GUIDE and we will DM you the link", lead capture forms, quiz flows. If what you actually want is for ordinary customer messages to get a quick, correct answer, it is the wrong shape of tool, and the alternatives are Meta Business Suite's free automated responses for simple cases, a social inbox such as our own Postd Office for answering real questions, or a broader social media suite if you also need reporting. ManyChat's Pro plan lists at $29 a month billed annually ($39 monthly), about £22 to £29 at current rates, so this is not mainly a price question. It is about what the tool is for.

We make Postd Office, so I will say where ManyChat or another tool is the better pick.

## What is ManyChat good at?

Credit where it is due. ManyChat is one of the best known chat automation tools, and it is very good at:

- **Comment-to-DM.** Someone comments a keyword on a Reel or post, and they get a DM with a link, a discount code or a download. For creators and online shops this can work very well.
- **Flows.** A visual builder for step-by-step conversations: ask a question, offer buttons, collect an email address, tag the contact.
- **Several channels.** Instagram, Facebook Messenger and WhatsApp, among others.
- **Campaigns.** Story replies, giveaways, product drops, sign-ups.

If you are a Derby bakery running a "comment CAKE for this week's order link" campaign every Friday, ManyChat is a sensible choice and nothing in this guide does that job better.

## How much does ManyChat cost?

ManyChat moved to pricing based on **active contacts** (the people you talk to each month) on 2 March 2026. As listed on [ManyChat's pricing page](https://manychat.com/pricing) and its [help centre](https://help.manychat.com/hc/en-us/articles/25800228332572), checked November 2026:

- **Pro:** from $29 a month billed annually, or $39 billed monthly, for up to 2,500 active contacts. That is about £22 to £29 a month at current rates, before VAT.
- There is a free plan and there are other tiers above and below Pro, with prices that rise as active contacts grow.

ManyChat's help pages were not all readable when I checked, and some review sites still show the older pricing, so check the pricing page with your own numbers. Accounts set up before March 2026 may still be on the old structure.

## Why do small businesses look for a ManyChat alternative?

These are the reasons owners give us, and they are mostly about fit rather than quality.

1. **Most of their messages are not campaigns.** A plumber in Mansfield does not get "comment BOILER" messages. He gets "Can you look at a dripping radiator valve in Clipstone on Thursday?". There is no keyword to catch, and no flow you could build in advance covers every version of that.
2. **The flow builder is a project.** Building, testing and maintaining flows takes time that a salon owner would rather spend with clients.
3. **Comments still need a person.** A comment-to-DM flow handles comments with your keyword. The complaint, the question about parking and the spam link under the same post still need dealing with somewhere.
4. **TikTok.** ManyChat has added TikTok, but its own [community posts](https://community.manychat.com/general-q-a-43/tiktok-eu-7420) in 2025 said the connection was not available in the UK or EU at first. Check the current position for your account.
5. **Bots that over-answer.** A badly set up flow can confidently send the wrong reply. Meta's [messaging policy](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/messenger-platform/policy) also requires automated chats to say they are automated where the law requires it, and recommends it everywhere.

## How to switch from ManyChat without losing anything

1. **Export your contacts** from ManyChat if you want to keep them for email marketing, and check you have permission to use them that way.
2. **Note which flows are still useful.** If one campaign genuinely earns money, keep ManyChat for that and nothing else.
3. **Turn off flows that answer general messages** before connecting another tool, so customers do not get two replies.
4. **Write down your approved answers:** prices, areas, hours, booking link, policies. Whatever tool you use next will be only as good as these.
5. **Set an instant reply and away message** in Meta Business Suite so nothing goes silent during the switch.
