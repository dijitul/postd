The best social media scheduler for a UK small business depends on one question: do you want a tool that posts what you write, or one that writes it too? If you are happy writing, Buffer or Publer will schedule to Google, Facebook and more for about £4 a month per channel, and Meta's and Google's own tools are free. If the writing is the part that never gets done, a tool that writes from your own business information (Localo, Semrush Local or postd, which we make) saves far more time than it costs.

This guide compares eleven options on what they cost in pounds, whether they post to Google Business Profile, how much of the writing they do, and who each one suits. Prices were checked on each vendor's own pricing page on 20 November 2026. Where a vendor shows US dollars, I have converted at about $1 to £0.76, so treat the pound figures as approximate. Prices exclude VAT unless stated.

A disclosure before we start: I run dijitul, the Mansfield agency that makes postd.uk and Postd Office. I have tried to be fair, and where another tool is the better pick I say so.

## What does a social media scheduling tool actually do?

A scheduler lets you write posts in one place, pick a date and time, and have them published for you on Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, Google Business Profile and so on. That is the core job, and every tool here does it.

On top of that, tools differ in four ways that matter to a small business:

1. **Which platforms they reach.** Google Business Profile is the one many general tools treat as an afterthought, yet for a plumber or a salon it is often where customers actually look.
2. **How they charge.** Per channel, per user, per workspace or per location. The cheapest headline price is not always the cheapest bill.
3. **How much writing they do.** From nothing, through "AI helps you rewrite your caption", to "here are this week's posts, please approve them".
4. **What else is bundled.** Inboxes for comments and messages, analytics, approval workflows, review replies.

## Which tools are best for a one-person business?

If you run the business and the social media yourself, the per-user and per-workspace tools are built for someone else. These are the realistic shortlist.

### Buffer: the simple, cheap scheduler

Buffer is the tool most people try first, and for good reason. It is clean, easy to learn, and priced per channel: [$5 per channel a month on Essentials](https://buffer.com/pricing), about £3.80, or $10 on Team if you need more than one user. A Nottingham salon posting to Facebook, Instagram and Google would pay about £11.40 a month.

It posts to Google Business Profile, supporting [What's New, Offer and Event posts](https://support.buffer.com/article/557-using-google-business-profiles-with-buffer), one image per post, no video. Its AI Assistant helps you write and repurpose, but you bring the idea.

**Pick Buffer if** you enjoy writing posts, you post to Instagram or TikTok as well, and you mainly want them to go out on time. More detail in [Buffer alternatives for UK small businesses](/guides/buffer-alternatives).

### Publer: the most for the least

Publer has a free plan for up to three accounts (not X) and paid plans [from $5 a month for one account](https://publer.com/help/en/article/what-are-publers-plans-and-pricing-15h4yqh/) on Professional, or $10 on Business, which adds unlimited AI Assist prompts. Annual billing takes 20% off.

It handles Google Business Profile well, including updates, events and offers, but its [help centre says](https://publer.com/help/en/article/managing-google-business-profiles-in-publer-rgtjud/) the profile must belong to a business with fewer than 10 locations, and video is not supported. It also refuses duplicate posts, in line with Google's rules on repeated content.

**Pick Publer if** cost is the deciding factor and you are comfortable with a busier interface.

### Meta Business Suite and Google's own scheduler: free, but separate

[Meta Business Suite](https://www.facebook.com/business/tools/meta-business-suite) is Meta's free tool for Facebook Pages and Instagram. It schedules posts and gives you one inbox for both. Google Business Profile has had its own [Schedule this post and Repeats options](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7342169?hl=en-GB) since late 2025, as covered in our [guide to Google Business Profile posts](/guides/google-business-profile-posts).

Between them, you can schedule to Facebook, Instagram and Google for nothing. The cost is that you do everything twice, in two places, and you still write every word.

**Pick the free tools if** you post once or twice a week and have the discipline to sit down and batch them.

## Which tools suit a team or an agency?

### Hootsuite: powerful, priced for teams

Hootsuite is a full social media management platform: publishing, a shared inbox, listening, reporting and AI tools. Its plans start at [$99 per user a month billed annually](https://www.hootsuite.com/plans), about £75, or $149 month to month (about £113). Professional is $199 a user a month on annual billing, about £151.

That is fair value for a marketing team that uses the inbox and reports every day. For a Derby café with one person posting three times a week, it is several times the cost of anything else here.

### Sprout Social: the premium end

Sprout Social's [pricing page](https://sproutsocial.com/pricing/) lists Essentials at $79 per seat a month on annual billing (about £60) or $99 monthly, then Standard at $199, Professional at $299 and Advanced at $399 per seat a month. It publishes to Google Business Profile, including updates, offers and events, according to its [help centre](https://support.sproutsocial.com/hc/en-us/articles/360041901331-Google-My-Business-Publishing), and pulls Google reviews into its [review management](https://sproutsocial.com/features/reviews/) feature. There is a 30-day free trial.

It is excellent software for teams that need reporting for a board or clients. It is not built for a sole trader's budget.

### Planable: for approvals

[Planable](https://planable.io/pricing/) prices per workspace with unlimited users: $33 a month on Basic (about £25) and $49 on Pro (about £37), with yearly billing giving two months free. Basic allows 60 posts a workspace a month across up to 4 pages. Its strength is collaboration: comments on the post itself and approval steps, with required approvals on Pro and multi-level approvals on Enterprise. It supports Google Business Profile scheduling.

**Pick Planable if** someone else has to sign off your posts, like a client, a franchise head office or a business partner.

### SocialBee: categories and recycling

[SocialBee](https://socialbee.com/pricing/) starts at $29 a month (about £22) for 5 profiles and 1 user, rising to $49 for 10 profiles and $99 for 25. It posts to Google Business Profile on all plans. Its trademark is content categories: you sort posts into buckets (tips, offers, reviews) and it rotates them, recycling evergreen posts. Its AI Copilot drafts a strategy with ready-made posts, and there is a paid done-for-you service, ConciergeBee, with prices not listed.

**Pick SocialBee if** you like the idea of building a library of posts once and letting it cycle.

## Which tools write the posts for you?

Almost every tool above has an AI writing helper now. That is not the same as the tool writing your posts. With a helper, you still decide what to post about, type a prompt, check the output and schedule it. Three tools in this guide go further and generate posts from what they know about your business.

### Localo

[Localo](https://localo.com/pricing) is a local SEO tool built around Google Business Profile. Its Single Business plan, listed at 39 a month billed annually plus VAT (about £30), autogenerates and publishes Google posts and can also publish to Facebook. It also covers rankings and profile optimisation. There is a 14-day trial with no card and a 30-day money-back guarantee.

### Semrush Local

[Semrush Local](https://www.semrush.com/pricing/local/) costs £24.31 per location a month on Base and £48.62 on Pro, both billed annually. Base includes Google Business Profile management run by an AI agent, with AI-generated posts and automated photo posting. It makes most sense if you already use Semrush for SEO.

### postd (ours)

postd reads your website (services, about, FAQ and blog pages) and your real Google reviews, then writes posts in UK English, separately for each platform, and publishes them to Google Business Profile, Facebook Pages, LinkedIn Company Pages and X. You can approve each post or leave it on autopilot. It uses your own photos first and only makes an AI image when nothing fits.

Plans are Local at £15 a month (one location, any two of Google, Facebook and LinkedIn, up to 3 posts a week each), Growth at £29 (all four platforms, up to 7 a week each) and Agency at £59 (three locations), all plus VAT. Annual billing gives two months free.

What it does not do: it does not post to Instagram or TikTok, and it does not reply to reviews or messages. If Instagram is your main channel, Buffer or Publer will suit you better.

## How much time does each approach actually take?

Here is a worked example, so you can swap in your own numbers. These are assumptions, not survey results.

**The business:** a heating engineer in Mansfield who wants to post 3 times a week each on Google Business Profile, Facebook and LinkedIn. That is 9 posts a week, or about 39 a month.

**Assumptions:**

- Thinking of an idea, writing it, adapting it for the platform and finding a photo takes 10 minutes a post, even with an AI helper.
- Checking and approving a post that a tool has written takes 2 minutes.
- The owner's time is worth £40 an hour (a realistic figure for a UK tradesperson; use £30 to £50 for your own sums).

| Approach | Owner time a month | Value of that time at £40/hour | Tool cost a month | Total a month |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Free tools (Meta Business Suite plus Google) | 39 posts x 10 min, plus LinkedIn by hand = about 6.5 hours | £260 | £0 | £260 |
| Buffer Essentials, 3 channels | about 6.5 hours | £260 | about £11.40 | about £271 |
| Hootsuite Standard, 1 user, annual | about 6.5 hours | £260 | about £75 | about £335 |
| postd Growth (three platforms needs Growth) | 39 posts x 2 min = about 1.3 hours | £52 | £29 plus VAT | about £81 plus VAT |

The scheduler is not where the cost is. The writing is. A scheduler saves you the minutes spent logging in and pressing publish; it does not save the 10 minutes of thinking on a Thursday night after a day on the tools.

Equally, if you would spend that time writing anyway, or you enjoy it, or someone on your team does it as part of their job, a £4-a-channel scheduler is the sensible buy and paying for writing is wasted money. ## What about replying to comments and messages?

Posting is half the job. The other half is everything that comes back: Facebook comments, Instagram DMs, TikTok comments, Google reviews.

- Hootsuite and Sprout Social include a full inbox, and Buffer has community engagement features for replying to comments. Planable sells its inbox as an add-on.
- Meta Business Suite has a free inbox for Facebook and Instagram.

## How to choose: a quick decision guide

1. **You post rarely and will happily batch it:** use Meta Business Suite and Google's own scheduler. Free.
2. **You write your own posts and want them in one place:** Buffer or Publer, about £4 a month per channel.
3. **Someone has to approve posts before they go out:** Planable.
4. **You have a team handling lots of messages and reports:** Hootsuite or Sprout Social, if the budget allows.
5. **Google is your main channel and you care about local rankings tools too:** Localo or Semrush Local.
6. **The posts simply never get written:** a tool that writes them from your own business, such as postd.

## What to check before you sign up to any tool

- **Does it post to Google Business Profile directly**, and which post types (updates, offers, events)?
- **How does the price grow** when you add a platform, a user or a second location?
- **Does the free plan cover the platforms you need?** Some free plans exclude certain networks or cap scheduled posts.
- **Does the AI write from your business or from a blank prompt?** Generic AI posts read like everyone else's.
- **Can you cancel monthly?** Several of the cheaper headline prices are annual billing.
- **Is the price in pounds and does it include VAT?** Most US tools quote dollars before tax.
