Planable is one of the best tools for getting social posts approved: posts appear as they will look, people comment on them directly, and approvals can be optional, required or multi-level. If nobody else needs to sign off your posts, you are paying for a workflow you will not use. A simple scheduler such as Buffer or Publer costs a fraction of the price, and if the real problem is writing the posts, a tool like postd (which we make) writes them and lets you approve each one in a queue.

Disclosure: I run dijitul, which makes postd. Where Planable is the right tool I say so. Prices were checked on vendors' own pages in late 2026. Dollar prices are converted at about $1 to £0.76, so pound figures are approximate and before VAT.

## What does Planable do well?

Planable is built around collaboration. From its [pricing page](https://planable.io/pricing/):

| | Basic | Pro | Enterprise |
|---|---|---|---|
| Price per workspace a month | $33, about £25 | $49, about £37 | Quote |
| Users | Unlimited | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| Posts per workspace a month | 60 | 150 | Unlimited |
| Social pages per workspace | 4 | 10 | 50 |
| Approval workflow | None or optional | None, optional or required, plus bulk approval | Multi-level approvals |
| AI post generate and rewrite | Yes | Yes | Yes |

Yearly billing gives two months free. Analytics and a social inbox are paid add-ons per workspace (Planable's page showed two different prices for each when I checked, roughly $7.50 to $9 a month for the inbox and $12 to $14 for analytics). Google Business Profile is supported for scheduling and analytics.

The free trial is generous in one way: 50 posts with no time limit and no card. But X publishing is not available during the trial.

**Planable is the better choice if:**

- you are an agency, and clients need to see and approve posts before they go out
- a franchise head office signs off what local branches post
- two business partners both want a say on every post
- you need a clear record of who approved what

## Why would a small business look for an alternative?

1. **There is no approval chain.** A sole trader in Newark writing their own posts does not need comment threads and approval steps.
2. **Monthly post caps.** Basic allows 60 posts per workspace a month. That is plenty for one business on two or three platforms, but it is a limit others do not have.
3. **Add-ons cost extra.** If you want an inbox and analytics as well, the bill grows.
4. **It still does not write the posts.** The AI tools generate and rewrite, but you start each post.

### Buffer and Publer: the cheap, simple swap

If you just want your own posts to go out on time, [Buffer](https://buffer.com/pricing) ($5 per channel a month) and [Publer](https://publer.com/help/en/article/what-are-publers-plans-and-pricing-15h4yqh/) (from $5 a month per account, with a free plan for 3 accounts) both do the job at a fraction of Planable's price, and both publish to Google Business Profile. More in [Buffer alternatives](/guides/buffer-alternatives) and [Publer alternatives](/guides/publer-alternative).

### SocialBee: for a library of posts

[SocialBee](https://socialbee.com/pricing/) suits people who want to write a stock of posts once, sort them into categories and let them rotate. See [SocialBee alternatives](/guides/socialbee-alternative).

### Hootsuite and Sprout Social: if you are a bigger team

If you are leaving Planable because you need more, not less (a full inbox, listening, reporting), Hootsuite and Sprout Social are the step up, at a much higher price. See [Hootsuite alternatives](/guides/hootsuite-alternatives) and [Sprout Social alternatives](/guides/sprout-social-alternative).

### The free tools

[Meta Business Suite](https://www.facebook.com/business/tools/meta-business-suite) schedules Facebook and Instagram for free, and Google Business Profile has its own [scheduling and repeat options](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7342169?hl=en-GB). No approvals, no writing help, but no bill.

### postd: approval without the chain

postd, which we make, has an approval step, but it is a different kind. Planable's approvals are for one person checking what another person wrote. postd's approval queue is for you checking what postd wrote.

It reads your website and real Google reviews, writes posts in UK English for each platform, and puts them in a queue. You read them, edit any you want to change, and approve. Once you trust it, you can switch to autopilot. It publishes to Google Business Profile, Facebook Pages, LinkedIn Company Pages and X, and uses your own photos first.

What it does not do: Instagram or TikTok, multi-person approval chains, or replying to messages. An agency that needs clients to sign off posts should stay with Planable.

## Planable vs the alternatives: a worked example

Assumptions, to change for your own business:

- **The business:** an independent garage in Newark, owner-run, posting 3 times a week to Google Business Profile and 3 times a week to Facebook. About 26 posts a month, nobody else approving.
- **Writing a post yourself**, with AI help, including the idea and a photo: 10 minutes.
- **Checking a post a tool wrote:** 2 minutes.
- **The owner's time** is worth £35 an hour.

| | Planable Basic | Buffer Essentials, 2 channels | postd Local |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tool cost a month | $33, about £25 | $10, about £7.60 | £15 plus VAT |
| Owner time a month | 26 x 10 min = about 4.3 hours | about 4.3 hours | 26 x 2 min = about 0.9 hours |
| Value of that time at £35/hour | about £152 | about £152 | about £30 |
| Total | about £177 | about £160 | about £45 plus VAT |

For an owner with no approval chain, Planable and Buffer take the same writing time, and Buffer costs less. A tool that writes the posts saves the most time, as long as the posts it writes are good enough to approve without heavy editing, which is worth testing in a free trial before you commit.

If you are an agency running ten client workspaces with sign-off, none of this applies: Planable's workflow saves hours of email back and forth that this table does not count.

## Which Planable alternative should you pick?

- **You write and publish alone:** Buffer or Publer.
- **You want a rotating library of posts:** SocialBee.
- **You need a bigger team platform:** Hootsuite or Sprout Social.
- **You want posts written, with a chance to check them first:** postd.
- **Clients or partners must sign off every post:** stay with Planable.
