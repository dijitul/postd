Publer is one of the best-value schedulers you can buy: a free plan for three accounts, paid plans from about £3.80 a month, and proper Google Business Profile support. Most people who look for a Publer alternative are not after a cheaper scheduler. They are after one that writes the posts. For that, look at postd (which we make) for Google, Facebook, LinkedIn and X, or Localo and Semrush Local if Google is all you care about. If you just want a simpler scheduler, Buffer is the obvious swap.

As always: I run dijitul, the company behind postd, so weigh my view accordingly. Prices were checked on each vendor's pages in late 2026. Dollar prices are converted at about $1 to £0.76, so pound figures are approximate and before VAT.

## What does Publer do well?

Quite a lot, for the money. From [Publer's plans and pricing page](https://publer.com/help/en/article/what-are-publers-plans-and-pricing-15h4yqh/):

| Plan | Price | About | Includes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | £0 | Up to 3 accounts (not X), 1 user, 10 scheduled posts per account at a time |
| Professional | From $5 a month | £3.80 | 1 account, more for each extra one, 7-day trial |
| Business | From $10 a month | £7.60 | 1 account, more for each extra one, unlimited AI Assist, 14-day trial |
| Enterprise | Quote | | Everything in Business |

Annual billing saves 20%, and for every nine accounts or members you add, the tenth is free.

On Google Business Profile, [Publer supports](https://publer.com/help/en/article/managing-google-business-profiles-in-publer-rgtjud/) updates, events, offers and photo uploads. It also guards against Google's rules: duplicate posts are blocked, and if you want to recycle a Google post you have to write at least 10 variations. That is more careful than many tools.

**Stick with Publer if** you are happy writing your posts, you want lots of networks for very little, and you do not mind a busy interface.

## Why look for a Publer alternative?

1. **The writing is still yours.** AI Assist helps with a caption once you have an idea, but it does not know your business, your recent jobs or your reviews unless you tell it.
2. **Bigger Google estates.** Publer's Google connection is for businesses with fewer than 10 locations, so a larger chain needs something else.
3. **No video on Google.** Publer's help page says video uploads to Google are not supported because of API limits.
4. **You prefer a calmer interface.** Some people find Buffer easier to live with day to day.

### Buffer

[Buffer](https://buffer.com/pricing) costs about the same as Publer ($5 per channel a month on Essentials) and covers Google with updates, offers and events. It is simpler to look at, and its free plan also covers three channels. See [Buffer alternatives](/guides/buffer-alternatives) for the full picture.

### SocialBee

[SocialBee](https://socialbee.com/pricing/) costs more, from $29 a month, and is built around content categories that rotate on a schedule. Its AI Copilot can draft a strategy and a set of posts. Covered in more depth in [SocialBee alternatives](/guides/socialbee-alternative).

### Planable

[Planable](https://planable.io/pricing/) is for teams: per-workspace pricing with unlimited users, comments on each post and approval steps. Overkill for a sole trader, very handy for an agency.

### Localo and Semrush Local

Both generate Google posts for you. [Localo](https://localo.com/pricing) autogenerates and publishes Google posts on its Single Business plan and can post to Facebook. [Semrush Local](https://www.semrush.com/pricing/local/) Base, at £24.31 per location a month on annual billing, includes Google Business Profile management run by an AI agent, with AI-generated posts and automated photo posting. Both also offer local ranking and listings tools that Publer does not. We compare Semrush Local in more detail in [Semrush Local alternatives](/guides/semrush-local-alternative).

### postd

postd, which we make, writes the posts rather than just sending them. It reads your website (services, about, FAQ and blog pages) and your real Google reviews, then writes a separate post for each platform in UK English, rotating between review quotes, service spotlights, tips, FAQs, local and behind-the-scenes angles. It publishes to Google Business Profile, Facebook Pages, LinkedIn Company Pages and X, at sensible local times, and you can approve each one or let it run on autopilot. ## Publer vs a tool that writes the posts: a worked example

Assumptions, to swap for your own:

- **The business:** a builder in Chesterfield posting 3 times a week each on Google Business Profile, Facebook and LinkedIn. That is 9 posts a week, about 39 a month.
- **Tool:** Publer's free plan covers the three accounts, as long as you top up the queue before it hits 10 scheduled posts per account.
- **Writing a post yourself**, with AI help, including the idea and a photo: 10 minutes.
- **Checking a post a tool has written:** 2 minutes.
- **The owner's time** is worth £40 an hour.

| | Publer free plan | postd Growth (3 platforms needs Growth) |
|---|---|---|
| Tool cost a month | £0 | £29 plus VAT |
| Owner time a month | 39 x 10 min = 6.5 hours | 39 x 2 min = 1.3 hours |
| Value of that time at £40/hour | £260 | £52 |
| Total | about £260 | about £81 plus VAT |

If you only post to two of those platforms at 3 a week, postd Local at £15 plus VAT covers it.

The point is not that Publer is poor value. It is excellent value as a scheduler. The cost is the writing, and only you know whether those six and a half hours happen. If they do, and you like doing them, Publer is the cheapest sensible answer. If the queue tends to sit empty, a free scheduler does not help.
