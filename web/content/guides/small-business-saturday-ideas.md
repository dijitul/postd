Small Business Saturday UK falls on Saturday 5 December 2026, confirmed on the [official Small Business Saturday UK website](https://smallbusinesssaturdayuk.com/). It is a free campaign to get people buying from small, local businesses, and you can join in with nothing more than a few honest posts. The posts that work best are about people, place and suppliers rather than discounts. Below are 15 ideas with copy-and-paste examples, a two-week plan starting today, and how to use a Google event post.

## What is Small Business Saturday UK?

It is a grassroots, non-commercial campaign held on the first Saturday of December, founded in the UK by American Express, which remains its main supporter. The idea is simple: encourage people to shop small and local, in person and online, on that day and beyond.

This year the campaign replaced its long-running "SmallBiz100" with the Nation's Favourite Businesses, featuring a different small business every day in the 100 days before 5 December. Applications for 2026 closed on 30 June 2026 ([Business Wales notice](https://businesswales.gov.wales/news-and-blog/nationss-favourite-businesses-2026)), so that is one to put in the diary for next year.

What you can still use now: the campaign's free marketing pack (logos, social media graphics and campaign materials) on the official site, and the hashtag **#SmallBizSatUK**.

## When should you start posting?

About two weeks out, which is today. People make Saturday plans during the week, so a post on the day itself is your weakest one. Here is a light plan of six posts.

| Date | Post | Idea numbers below |
|---|---|---|
| Sun 22 Nov or Mon 23 Nov | "We are taking part" | 1 |
| Thu 26 Nov | Meet the team | 2, 3 |
| Tue 1 Dec | Supplier shout-out | 6, 7 |
| Thu 3 Dec | What is on Saturday | 10, 11 |
| Sat 5 Dec | Today's the day | 12 |
| Mon 7 Dec | Thank you | 15 |

If you are also running something for Black Friday on 27 November, keep the two separate. Black Friday is about a deal. Small Business Saturday is about you. Our [Black Friday guide](/guides/black-friday-ideas-small-business-uk) has a separate plan for that week.

## 15 post ideas for Small Business Saturday

### Ideas about you

1. **Say you are taking part.** One line on why you started the business, and what you will be doing on 5 December.
2. **Meet the owner.** A real photo, your name, how long you have been going, what you did before.
3. **Meet the team.** Who answers the phone, who does the cuts, who makes the cakes.
4. **A number from your year.** Only true ones. "This year we fitted 212 boilers in Mansfield and Sutton." If you do not count it, do not post it.
5. **Behind the scenes.** The van at 7am, the kitchen before opening, the stockroom.

Example for a salon in Nottingham:

> Small Business Saturday is on 5 December, so here is who we are. I opened the salon eight years ago after working for someone else for twelve. There are four of us now: me, Jess, Priya and Sam on Saturdays. We will be open 9 till 4 on the day, with mince pies and a few walk-in slots for fringe trims. #SmallBizSatUK

### Ideas about other local businesses

6. **Your suppliers.** The bakery that makes your bread, the merchant who has your parts on the shelf.
7. **Your neighbours.** Three businesses on your street worth a visit on the day.
8. **A trade you recommend.** A plumber who always sends people to the same electrician can say so.
9. **A joint offer or trail.** Shops on the same street running a stamp card or a map.

Example for a café in Derby:

> Small Business Saturday is about all of us, so here are three of our neighbours. Our bread comes from the bakery two doors down, baked at 4am. Our milk is from a farm 12 miles away. And the florist opposite does the flowers on our tables every Monday. If you are coming in on 5 December, pop next door too. #SmallBizSatUK

### Ideas for the day itself

10. **What is happening.** Opening hours, a tasting, a demo, extended hours.
11. **A genuine offer, if you want one.** A free extra or a small discount, with a clear end time. The usual price rules apply: no invented "was" prices.
12. **"We are open" on the morning.** A photo of the door open and the lights on.
13. **Live updates.** One or two through the day, no more. "Last two loaves of sourdough."
14. **A thank you to a customer.** A real review, quoted word for word, first name only.

Example for a plumber in Mansfield (no shop, still taking part):

> We do not have a shop to open on Small Business Saturday, but we are a small business all the same: two vans, three engineers, all from Mansfield. If you have used us this year, the best thing you can do for us on 5 December is leave a Google review or tell a neighbour. Thank you. #SmallBizSatUK

### After the day

15. **Thank everyone.** What happened, who came in, a photo. And a gentle nudge about Christmas hours or January bookings.

Example:

> Thank you to everyone who came in on Saturday. We sold out of mince pies by 1pm, which has never happened before. Christmas opening hours are on our profile now, and we are taking January bookings.

## How do you post Small Business Saturday on Google Business Profile?

If you are doing something on the day (a tasting, late opening, a demo), use an **event** post. [Google's help page on posts](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7342169?hl=en-GB) says an event needs a title and start and end dates and times. If you leave the times out, it shows as lasting all day.

1. Start a new post on your profile and choose the event type.
2. Title: "Small Business Saturday: mince pies and late opening".
3. Dates: Saturday 5 December 2026, with your real times.
4. Description: one or two sentences about what is on.
5. Add a real photo and a button (Learn more, Book or Order).

Leave phone numbers out of the text, since Google's [posts content policy](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7213077?hl=en-GB) says posts with contact details may be removed. If you are only posting a "we are taking part" message, a normal update is fine. More on post types in our [guide to Google Business Profile posts](/guides/google-business-profile-posts).

## What should you avoid?

- **Hashtag stuffing.** One #SmallBizSatUK is enough. Strings of #shoplocal #supportsmall #smallbusiness look like spam.
- **Posts that are only a discount.** The day is about why people should choose you. A "10% off" graphic says nothing about that.
- **Stock photos.** The campaign graphics are fine for one post. Everything else should be your own photos.
- **Tagging accounts you have not asked.** If you shout out a neighbour, it is polite to tell them first, and they may share it.
- **Going quiet afterwards.** The people who find you on 5 December will look at your page again in January. Give them something to see.
