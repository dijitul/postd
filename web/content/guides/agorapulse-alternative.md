The best Agorapulse alternative for a small business depends on how many people answer your messages. Agorapulse charges per user, so every member of staff on the inbox adds £59 to £149 a month. Postd Office is a flat £49 a month plus VAT with unlimited seats, for Facebook and Instagram messages and comments and TikTok comments, with AI-drafted replies you approve. Meta Business Suite is free for Facebook and Instagram. Agorapulse remains the better choice if you need LinkedIn, X or Google reviews in the same inbox.

A disclosure: we make Postd Office, and its sister product postd. I have tried to be fair to Agorapulse, which is a good product with a well-made inbox.

## What is Agorapulse?

Agorapulse is a social media management tool used by agencies and marketing teams. It covers publishing, reporting and listening, but its reputation is built on its **inbox**: comments, DMs and reviews from your connected networks in one place, which you work through until it is empty.

## How much does Agorapulse cost?

Agorapulse charges **per user**. Prices on its [pricing page](https://www.agorapulse.com/pricing/) are shown in dollars (checked December 2026). I have converted at roughly 75p to the dollar, so treat the pound figures as approximate and before any VAT.

| Plan | Per user, billed annually | Per user, billed monthly | About in pounds | Social profiles |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Standard | $79 | $99 | about £59, or £74 monthly | 10 |
| Professional | $119 | $149 | about £89, or £112 monthly | 10 |
| Advanced | $149 | $199 | about £112, or £149 monthly | 10 |
| Custom | Quote | Quote | | Unlimited |

Extra social profiles cost $10 a month each on annual billing or $15 on monthly. There is a 30-day free trial with no card needed, and a free plan you can stay on afterwards, although the pricing page does not spell out its limits.

## What does the Agorapulse inbox do well?

- **It covers a lot of networks.** The pricing page says the inbox handles comments, DMs and reviews from all connected networks, and Google Business Profile reviews moderation is on every plan.
- **It is on every paid plan.** Unlike some rivals, you do not have to jump to a mid-tier plan to get it.
- **Translation.** Every plan can translate incoming messages inside the inbox.
- **Team features further up.** Professional adds assigning items to a team member, reply collision detection and ad comments. Advanced adds saved replies, labels and bulk actions.
- **Inbox rules.** Each paid plan includes one automation rule per social profile; the Custom plan allows unlimited rules.

On TikTok, Agorapulse's [help centre](https://support.agorapulse.com/en/articles/10393075-agorapulse-inbox-explained) is clear that it covers comments but **not messages**, because of TikTok's API limits. It can hide TikTok comments but not delete them.

## Why do small businesses look for an alternative?

1. **The per-user bill.** A salon where the owner and three stylists all answer Instagram DMs needs four users. On Professional, so you can assign messages, that is four times $119.
2. **The good team features are higher up.** Assigning messages starts on Professional, saved replies on Advanced, and AI reply suggestions only on Custom.
3. **Replies are still typed by hand.** On the plans most small businesses would buy, someone writes every answer.
4. **It is built for marketers.** Ten profiles, reporting and listening are useful for an agency. A café with one Facebook Page and one Instagram account uses a fraction of it.

## When is Agorapulse the better choice?

- You need **LinkedIn, X or Google reviews** in the same inbox as Facebook and Instagram.
- You run **several brands or clients**. Agorapulse's 10 profiles per plan suit an agency; Postd Office is one brand per plan.
- You want **reports and listening** alongside the inbox.
- Only **one person** answers messages, so per-user pricing does not hurt.

## When is Postd Office the better choice?

- **Several people** answer messages and you do not want to pay for each one.
- Most enquiries arrive as **Facebook and Instagram DMs**, and the same questions come up again and again: prices, availability, parking, opening hours.
- You want **replies drafted for you**, but never sent without someone approving them.
- You get messages in **other languages** and want to reply in them.

## What does it cost a small team? A worked example

Take a hair salon in Leicester. The owner and three stylists answer Instagram and Facebook messages between clients, about 200 messages a month.

**Assumptions:** prices before VAT, annual billing for Agorapulse, dollars at roughly 75p. Typing a reply from scratch takes 3 minutes; checking and approving a drafted reply takes 1 minute. These are assumptions for the example, not measurements. Staff time is valued at £15 an hour.

| Setup | Tool cost a month | Reply time a month | Value of reply time | Total a month |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Agorapulse Standard, 4 users | 4 x $79 = $316, about £237 | 200 x 3 min = 10 h | £150 | about £387 |
| Agorapulse Professional, 4 users (to assign messages) | 4 x $119 = $476, about £357 | 10 h | £150 | about £507 |
| Postd Office Sorting Office | £49 | 200 x 1 min = 3 h 20 min | £50 | about £99 |
| Meta Business Suite, free | £0 | 10 h | £150 | £150 |

Not every message will have an approved answer, especially in the first few weeks, so the real time saving with drafts will be smaller at first and grow as you approve more answers. Even ignoring time completely, the seat cost alone is £49 against about £237 a month. If the same salon also wanted Google posts, adding postd Local at £15 a month would cover Google Business Profile and one other platform.

## Where to start

1. Count the people who answer messages, and the networks the messages come from.
2. If LinkedIn, X or Google reviews are a big part of it, trial Agorapulse.
3. If it is Facebook, Instagram and TikTok, and more than one person answers, compare a flat-price inbox.
4. Write down your ten most common questions and their answers. Whatever tool you pick, that list is what makes fast replies possible.
