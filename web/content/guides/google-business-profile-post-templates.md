Below are 25 Google Business Profile post templates you can copy, fill in and publish today: seven for trades, six for salons, six for cafés and six for professional services. Each one has the post type, a suggested button and words in [square brackets] to replace with your own details. None of them includes a phone number in the text, because Google may remove posts that do, so use the Call now button instead.

## How to use these templates

Each template follows the same pattern that works on Google: the useful fact first, a believable detail in the middle, one next step at the end. When you fill one in:

1. **Replace every bracket with something specific.** "[area]" becomes "Kirkby-in-Ashfield", not "the local area".
2. **Add a real photo.** Your own work, your own team, your own premises.
3. **Pick one button** from those Google offers: Book, Order online, Buy, Learn more, Sign up or Call now.
4. **Read it out loud.** If it sounds like a brochure, cut words until it sounds like you.
5. **Check for contact details.** No phone numbers, email addresses or social handles in the text. Google's [posts content policy](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7213077?hl=en-GB) explains why.

## Templates for trades

These suit plumbers, electricians, builders, roofers, joiners and anyone else who works at the customer's address. For a full monthly plan, see [social media for plumbers](/guides/social-media-for-plumbers).

### 1. The finished job (update, Call now)

> Just finished [job] in [area]. The problem was [what was wrong], which is [common/unusual] in [type of property]. We [what you did] and [result, for example: had the heating back on by teatime]. Covering [area], [area] and [area]. Tap Call now if you have the same problem.

### 2. The hidden cause (update, Learn more)

> Called out to [symptom the customer noticed] in [area] this week. The real cause was [what you found], tucked away [where it was]. Fixing the symptom would have meant another call-out in a month. Worth knowing if you have noticed [symptom] at home.

### 3. The seasonal reminder (update, Call now)

> [Season or month] is when we see the most [type of job]. If your [appliance or part of the house] [warning sign], now is the time to get it looked at, before [what happens if left]. Booking [type of job] across [area] for [month].

### 4. The credentials post (update, Learn more)

> Every job we do is [certified/registered/guaranteed] under [scheme, for example Gas Safe, NICEIC, NAPIT or TrustMark]. You can check our registration yourself on the [scheme] website. It takes a minute. We leave you [certificate or paperwork] at the end of every job.

Only use a scheme you genuinely belong to. Google's content rules prohibit false or misleading claims.

### 5. The before-you-book tip (update, Call now)

> Before we arrive for [type of job], it helps if you [simple preparation, for example: clear the cupboard under the sink]. It saves us [time] and saves you [money or mess]. Booking [type of job] in [area] for [next week/this month].

### 6. The diary update (update, Call now)

> We have [number] slots left for [type of job] before [date or holiday]. After that the diary is full until [date]. If you have been putting off [job], tap Call now.

### 7. The offer (offer post, View offer added automatically)

> Title: [Amount or percentage] off [service]
>
> [Service] for [price or discount] when you book before [date]. Includes [what is included]. Covering [area] and [area].
>
> Terms: Bookings made by [date]. [Any limit, for example: one per household]. Not with any other offer.

## Templates for professional services

These suit accountants, bookkeepers, solicitors, estate agents, surveyors, architects and consultants.

### 20. The deadline reminder (update, Learn more)

> Reminder: [deadline, for example: the online Self Assessment deadline] is [date]. If you [who it affects], you need to [action] by then. We have [number] spaces for new clients this [month]. Tap Learn more for our fixed fees.

Check every deadline against the official source (gov.uk for HMRC dates) before you post it.

### 21. The plain-English explainer (update, Learn more)

> We get asked about [term] a lot. In plain English: [one-sentence explanation]. It matters to you if [situation]. [One line on what to do next].

### 22. The common question (update, Learn more)

> "[Question a client asked, in their words]" is one of the questions we hear most. The short answer: [answer]. The longer answer depends on [factors], so [next step].

### 23. Meet the team (update, Learn more)

> [Name] looks after [type of client or work] here. [One line on background or qualification]. Outside work, [one human detail, if they are happy to share it]. If you [situation], [name] is who you will deal with.

### 24. The review quote (update, Learn more)

> "[Review text, quoted word for word]" Thank you, [first name]. [One line about the job the review refers to]. [Next step for someone in the same situation].

Only quote real reviews, word for word. UK law has banned fake consumer reviews since April 2025, and Google's [content policy](https://support.google.com/contributionpolicy/answer/7400114?hl=en-GB) prohibits content not based on a real experience.

### 25. The seasonal opening hours (update, Learn more)

> Our [holiday] hours: [day and date] [hours], [day and date] closed, back to normal on [day and date]. [One line on what to do before then, for example: send us your paperwork by 18 December].

Set the same dates under special hours as well. Our guide to [Christmas opening hours on Google Business Profile](/guides/google-business-profile-christmas-hours) has the UK 2026 bank holiday dates and the steps.

## Which template should you use each week?

Rotating the type of post keeps your profile varied. One simple pattern for one post a week:

| Week | Trades | Salons | Cafés | Professional services |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1 (finished job) | 9 (new look) | 14 (today's special) | 21 (explainer) |
| 2 | 4 (credentials) | 10 (aftercare tip) | 16 (practical information) | 24 (review quote) |
| 3 | 3 (seasonal reminder) | 8 (last-minute gap) | 18 (behind the scenes) | 22 (common question) |
| 4 | 7 (offer) | 13 (midweek offer) | 17 or 19 (event or offer) | 20 (deadline) |

Why weekly? It is a sensible minimum that keeps something current on your profile, and the reasons are in [how often to post on Google Business Profile](/guides/how-often-post-google-business-profile). ## Five mistakes that ruin a good template

1. **Leaving the template showing.** "We offer [service] in [area]" with the brackets still in has been published more often than you would think. Read it back before you post.
2. **Filling the brackets with vague words.** "Great results for local customers" tells nobody anything. Name the street, the job, the product.
3. **Adding your phone number "just in case".** It is the most common reason a post is rejected. The Call now button does the same job.
4. **Using the same filled-in post twice.** Google's [content rules](https://support.google.com/contributionpolicy/answer/7400114?hl=en-GB) list repetitive content as prohibited. Change the job, the area and the photo.
5. **A stock photo.** It undoes the believable detail you wrote. A clear phone photo of your own work is better.
