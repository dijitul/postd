Meta Business Suite's inbox is free and brings Facebook Page messages and comments together with Instagram DMs and comments, which is enough for a lot of small businesses. What it misses: TikTok altogether, replies that use your own information rather than fixed text, and simple team access for staff who do not want to wrestle with Meta's permissions. The alternatives range from per-user social media suites such as Agorapulse and Sprout Social (roughly £60 to £150 a user a month at current rates) to inboxes built for small businesses, such as our own Postd Office at £49 a month + VAT.

We make Postd Office, so this guide says plainly when the free option, or someone else's tool, is the better choice.

## What does the Meta Business Suite inbox do well?

Quite a lot, for nothing.

- **Facebook and Instagram in one list.** Meta's help page on [managing messages with Inbox](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/294426838452244) describes one place for messages and comments from your Facebook Page and Instagram account. You can connect WhatsApp too.
- **Basic organisation.** Mark conversations as done, move them to spam, follow up later, and add labels.
- **Automated responses.** An instant reply to first messages, an away message, suggested replies to up to five frequently asked questions, and custom keyword replies on desktop, as set out in Meta's [automated responses page](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/395965998733706).
- **Inbox suggestions.** Meta can [suggest replies](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/991413128263758) during a conversation, which you accept or ignore.
- **Phone and computer.** There is a Meta Business Suite app as well as the desktop site.
- **It is Meta's own.** No extra connection to set up, and new Meta features tend to appear there first.

If that list covers your week, stay with it (the wider choice is set out in [one inbox for Facebook, Instagram and TikTok messages](/guides/social-media-inbox-small-business)). Seriously. A Mansfield plumber who gets a few Facebook messages a day and answers them himself does not need to pay for anything.

## What does Meta Business Suite's inbox miss?

These are the gaps that come up most often when small businesses ask us about alternatives.

### 1. No TikTok

Meta Business Suite only covers Meta's platforms. If you post on TikTok, its comments live in the TikTok app, so you are back to checking two places. For a salon or café that gets a lot of comments on TikTok videos, this is the main reason to look elsewhere.

### 2. Automatic replies are fixed text

Instant replies, away messages, FAQ presets and keyword replies all send words you wrote in advance. Keyword replies only fire on an exact match, and Meta's help page says that if a preset (such as an away message) is triggered at the same time, the preset is sent instead. That is fine for "What are your opening hours?". It cannot answer "Can you fit a balayage in on Saturday afternoon and how much would it be for shoulder-length hair?".

Inbox suggestions help, but they are suggestions from Meta. They are not drawn from a list of answers you have approved.

### 3. Team access goes through Meta's permissions

To let staff answer messages, you add them to your business portfolio and give them access to the Page and Instagram account. Each person needs their own Facebook account. For a cleaner with a part-time office helper who does not use Facebook, or a café with Saturday staff, this is where it gets awkward, and it is why so many small businesses still share the owner's password. That is a security risk and makes it impossible to see who said what.

### 4. Hard to see who did what

When two or three people reply from the same inbox, it is not always obvious who answered a customer or what was promised. We have not found a simple activity log in Meta's inbox help pages.

### 5. Spam and scams sit with real customers

Fake "your Page will be disabled" messages, spam links in comments and the odd abusive comment arrive in the same inbox as genuine enquiries. You can move things to spam and hide comments, and Facebook's [keyword filter](https://www.facebook.com/help/131671940241729) hides comments with words you choose, but there is no clear flag for "this one looks like a scam" or "this one is urgent".

### 6. It changes, and sometimes breaks

Meta updates Business Suite often, and features move. It also has bad days: in May 2026 a bug stopped people sending messages from the Business Suite inbox on the web for a time, as [reported by PiunikaWeb](https://piunikaweb.com/2026/05/07/meta-business-suite-messages-not-sending/). Free tools do not come with anyone to ring.

### When Agorapulse or Sprout Social is the better choice

If you have a marketing person who needs scheduling, reporting and the inbox in one place, across several networks including LinkedIn and X, these are mature, well-supported tools. Agorapulse has long made its shared inbox a central feature. You pay for that breadth, per person, so they make most sense for teams where one or two people do all the social media work.

If your main problem is volume of comments (a busy restaurant or a brand that runs ads), NapoleonCat's auto-moderation rules are worth a trial. Its [pricing page](https://napoleoncat.com/pricing/) says the free trial includes the Social Inbox, auto-moderation and TikTok.

## Is it worth paying to replace a free inbox?

Do the sum with your own numbers. A worked example, with stated assumptions, for a dog groomer in Chesterfield with an owner and one assistant:

- 30 messages and comments a week across Facebook, Instagram and TikTok.
- Checking TikTok separately adds about 10 minutes a day, 6 days a week: 1 hour a week.
- Writing replies from scratch takes about 3 minutes each; checking and sending a drafted reply takes about 1 minute. That is 90 minutes against 30, saving 1 hour a week.
- The owner's time is worth £35 an hour.

Saved: 2 hours a week x £35 = £70 a week, about £303 a month (£70 x 52 ÷ 12). The inbox costs £58.80 a month including VAT. If your numbers look like that, it pays. If you get five messages a week and no TikTok comments, it does not, and Meta Business Suite is the right answer.
