SocialBee is a good scheduler for people who like to build a library of posts, sort them into categories and let them rotate. The main alternatives are Buffer or Publer if you want something simpler and cheaper, Planable if posts need approving, and Localo or postd if you would rather the tool wrote the posts from your own business than recycled ones you wrote months ago.

Disclosure first: I run dijitul, which makes postd. I have tried to be fair to SocialBee and say when it is the better choice. All prices were checked on the vendors' own pages in late 2026 and converted from US dollars at about $1 to £0.76, so pound figures are approximate and before VAT.

## What does SocialBee do well?

SocialBee's big idea is **content categories**. You make buckets such as "tips", "reviews", "offers" and "behind the scenes", add posts to each, and set a weekly schedule of which category goes out when. Evergreen posts can be recycled, so the queue never runs dry.

On top of that, according to its [pricing page](https://socialbee.com/pricing/):

- **Lots of networks**, including Google Business Profile locations on all plans.
- **Unlimited AI content generation** for captions and images, and an **AI Copilot** that drafts a social media strategy with ready-to-use posts.
- **A done-for-you option.** Its agency, ConciergeBee, offers content creation, community management and ads, with no prices published.
- **A 14-day free trial** with no card.

**SocialBee is the better choice if** you enjoy planning content, have a decent stock of good evergreen posts, and post to Instagram, TikTok or Pinterest as well as Google and Facebook.

## Why look for a SocialBee alternative?

1. **You do not have a library to recycle.** Categories are only as good as what you put in them. If you never sat down to write 40 posts, the rota has nothing to rotate.
2. **Recycling and Google do not mix well.** Google's [posts content policy](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7213077?hl=en-GB) treats repetitive content as prohibited. Some schedulers enforce this themselves: Publer's [help centre](https://publer.com/help/en/article/managing-google-business-profiles-in-publer-rgtjud/) says duplicate Google posts are not allowed. If you recycle to Google, change the wording each time.
3. **Price for a single business.** £22 a month is not much, but a one-van business posting to two platforms can pay less elsewhere.
4. **You want approvals.** SocialBee's Bootstrap and Accelerate plans have one user, so sign-off from someone else is awkward.

### Buffer: simpler and cheaper

[Buffer](https://buffer.com/pricing) has no categories, but it is easy, posts to Google Business Profile (updates, offers and events), and costs $5 per channel a month on Essentials. Three channels is about £11.40 a month. Its free plan covers 3 channels with 10 scheduled posts each. More in [Buffer alternatives](/guides/buffer-alternatives).

### Publer: cheapest, with recycling of its own

[Publer](https://publer.com/help/en/article/what-are-publers-plans-and-pricing-15h4yqh/) has a free plan for 3 accounts, and paid plans from $5 a month per account. It can recycle content too, and for Google it insists you write at least 10 variations so the same words are not posted twice. That is a sensible guard rail.

### Planable: for sign-off

[Planable](https://planable.io/pricing/) prices per workspace with unlimited users, from $33 a month. You see each post as it will appear, comment on it, and on Pro you can make approval required. Good for a salon owner who wants to check what a junior stylist has written before it goes on the salon's page.

### Localo: Google first

[Localo](https://localo.com/pricing) is a local SEO tool that autogenerates and publishes Google posts, can post to Facebook, and tracks your Google Maps rankings. A fuller comparison is in our [Localo alternatives guide](/guides/localo-alternative).

### Meta Business Suite plus Google: free

If your only channels are Facebook, Instagram and Google, you can schedule all three for nothing using [Meta Business Suite](https://www.facebook.com/business/tools/meta-business-suite) and Google's own [Schedule this post](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7342169?hl=en-GB) option. We look at whether Meta's tool is enough in [Meta Business Suite scheduler: is the free option enough?](/guides/meta-business-suite-vs-postd).

### postd: fresh posts instead of recycled ones

postd, which we make, takes a different approach to keeping the queue full. Instead of rotating old posts, it reads your website (services, about, FAQ and blog pages) and your real Google reviews and writes new posts each week, rotating angles: review quotes, service spotlights, tips, FAQs, local and behind the scenes. When a new page appears on your website, it writes a "what's new" post about it.

It publishes to Google Business Profile, Facebook Pages, LinkedIn Company Pages and X, with an approval queue or autopilot. It does not post to Instagram, TikTok or Pinterest, and it does not handle messages. If those networks matter to you, SocialBee or Buffer is the better fit.

## SocialBee vs a tool that writes the posts: a worked example

These are assumptions to adjust, not survey results.

**The business:** an electrician in Nottingham posting 3 times a week to Google Business Profile and 3 times a week to Facebook, so about 26 posts a month.

**Assumptions:**

- Setting up SocialBee properly takes an initial 6 hours to write a library of 40 evergreen posts across categories, then 1 hour a month topping it up and refreshing wording for Google.
- Writing a post from scratch each time takes 10 minutes.
- Checking a post a tool wrote takes 2 minutes.
- The owner's time is worth £40 an hour.

|---|---|---|---|
| Tool cost a month | $29, about £22 | $10, about £7.60 | £15 plus VAT |
| Owner time in month one | 6 hours setup + 1 hour = 7 hours | 26 x 10 min = about 4.3 hours | 26 x 2 min = about 0.9 hours |
| Owner time in later months | about 1 hour | about 4.3 hours | about 0.9 hours |
| Value of later-month time at £40/hour | £40 | about £173 | about £35 |
| Total in a typical later month | about £62 | about £181 | about £50 plus VAT |

Two things stand out. First, SocialBee's recycling genuinely does save time once the library exists, which is the whole point of it. Second, the result depends on that library being good and staying fresh: customers who follow you on Facebook will notice the same post coming round every eight weeks. A tool that writes new posts from your current website and latest reviews avoids that, at a similar monthly cost.

## Which SocialBee alternative should you choose?

- **You want simpler and cheaper:** Buffer or Publer.
- **You need someone to approve posts:** Planable.
- **Google Maps visibility is what you care about:** Localo.
- **You only use Facebook, Instagram and Google:** the free tools.
- **You do not want to write or maintain a library of posts:** postd.
- **You love categories and post to lots of networks:** stay with SocialBee.
