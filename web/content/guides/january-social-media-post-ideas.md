January is quiet for a lot of local businesses, which makes it a good month to be visible: fewer competitors are posting, and customers are planning the year. The dependable January topics are "we are open", Veganuary and Dry January for food and drink, fresh-start services, the Self Assessment deadline on 31 January 2027, and a genuine quiet-month offer. Below is a January 2027 calendar, 20 ideas grouped by type of business, and copy-and-paste example posts.

## What are the key January 2027 dates?

| Date | What | Who it suits |
|---|---|---|
| Fri 1 Jan | New Year's Day (bank holiday) | Opening hours, "see you on Monday" |
| Mon 4 Jan | Back to work; bank holiday in Scotland only | Everyone: "we are open" |
| All month | [Veganuary](https://veganuary.com/) | Cafés, restaurants, bakeries, delis |
| All month | Dry January, run by [Alcohol Change UK](https://alcoholchange.org.uk/blog/the-charity-behind-dry-january) | Pubs, bars, cafés, gyms |
| Mon 18 Jan | Blue Monday (third Monday) | Use lightly, if at all |
| Mon 25 Jan | Burns Night | Restaurants, pubs, butchers |
| Sun 31 Jan | Self Assessment online filing and payment deadline | Accountants, bookkeepers, sole traders |

Bank holiday dates are from [GOV.UK](https://www.gov.uk/bank-holidays). The Self Assessment deadline is 11:59pm on 31 January 2027 for 2025 to 2026 returns filed online, per [GOV.UK's deadlines page](https://www.gov.uk/self-assessment-tax-returns/deadlines).

A note on Blue Monday. It started as a press release for a holiday company in 2005 and has no basis in research. A cheerful "here is something nice on a grey Monday" post is fine. Tying an offer to people feeling low is not a good look.

## What should every business post in the first week?

1. **We are open.** Your first day back and your normal hours.
2. **The January diary is open.** Especially for trades and salons, where people book ahead.
3. **Thank you for last year.** Short, with a real number if you have one.
4. **What is new this year.** A new service, new hours, a new team member.
5. **A review from December.** Quoted word for word, first name only.

Example for a plumber in Mansfield:

> We are back from Monday 4 January. Boiler breakdowns are still our first priority, and the diary is open for services, bathroom refits and quotes. If the cold snap has found a weak spot in your heating, book using the button below.

Example for a salon in Nottingham:

> Happy New Year. We are open again from Tuesday 5 January, and there are plenty of January appointments if you want a fresh start after the party season. New this year: Saturday afternoons, until 4pm.

## Ideas for cafés, restaurants and pubs

6. **Veganuary dishes.** What is on, with a real photo. Even one good plant-based dish is worth a post.
7. **Alcohol-free options for people doing Dry January.** Low and no drinks, mocktails, good coffee.
8. **A quiet-night offer.** Two courses on a Tuesday, a midweek pie night, kids eat free.
9. **Burns Night on 25 January.** Haggis, neeps and tatties, a whisky flight or a vegetarian haggis.
10. **Warming food.** Soup of the day, a stew, a proper hot chocolate.

A quick note on names. Dry January is a registered trademark of Alcohol Change UK. Saying you have "plenty for anyone doing Dry January" is ordinary description. Calling your menu "the official Dry January menu" is not, unless you are a partner.

Example for a café in Derby:

> Doing Veganuary? Our new plant-based breakfast is on all month: smashed avocado, roasted tomatoes, mushrooms and spiced beans on sourdough, £9. Ask us about oat, soya or coconut milk for your coffee too.

Example for a pub:

> Plenty here for anyone having a dry January: three alcohol-free beers on draught, a proper ginger beer and our bartender's new alcohol-free spritz. Same pub, same fire, same quiz on a Thursday.

## Ideas for trades and home services

11. **Cold-weather problems.** Frozen condensate pipes, outside taps, draughty doors, damp.
12. **Planning season.** "Thinking about a new kitchen this year? Here is how long it really takes."
13. **January slots.** If January is your quietest month, say you have availability. People like a shorter wait.
14. **A job from the first week.** Proof you are back and busy.

Example for a heating engineer:

> If your boiler showed a fault during the freeze, check the condensate pipe first. It is the plastic pipe running outside from your boiler, and in cold weather it can freeze and stop the boiler. Pouring warm (not boiling) water along it often clears it. If that does not work, give us a call through the button below.

Example for a builder:

> If you want an extension or a new kitchen by the summer, now is when to get quotes in. Most of our jobs need four to eight weeks of planning before we start on site. We have a few January survey slots in Mansfield, Sutton and Kirkby.

## Ideas for salons, gyms and wellbeing businesses

15. **Fresh-start services.** A new look, a skin consultation, a beginners' class.
16. **A genuine January offer.** For your quietest days, with a clear end date.
17. **How to keep a resolution going.** Practical, not preachy: "two classes a week is a good start".

Example for a gym in Leicester:

> New to the gym this January? Our beginners' sessions run on Monday and Wednesday evenings all month. Small groups, no mirrors-and-muscles nonsense, and the coach will show you around everything. First session is free.

## Ideas for accountants and professional services

18. **Self Assessment countdown.** One post a week through January, getting more practical as 31 January gets closer.
19. **What you need to file.** A short checklist: UTR, income records, expenses, any pension or Gift Aid payments.
20. **Late-filing help.** If you take on last-minute clients, say so, and say when your cut-off is.

Example:

> Self Assessment reminder: the deadline to file your 2025 to 2026 tax return online and pay what you owe is 11:59pm on Sunday 31 January 2027. If you have not started, the first thing you need is your UTR and a list of your income for the year. We are taking on a small number of new sole trader clients until 20 January.

## Should you run a January sale?

If January is genuinely quiet for you, a short offer can help. Three rules keep it honest and effective:

- **Aim it at your slowest days or services**, not your whole price list. "Tuesday and Wednesday colours, 15% off" rather than "15% off everything".
- **Give it a real end date** and stick to it.
- **Only use a "was" price you have genuinely charged.** The ASA's [advice on savings claims](https://www.asa.org.uk/advice-online/promotional-savings-claims.html) says a "was" price must be the genuine usual selling price, and it generally expects at least three months of pricing history.

On Google Business Profile, use an offer post with the start and end dates set, so it disappears from view when the offer ends. Leave your phone number out of the post text; Google's [posts content policy](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7213077?hl=en-GB) says it may remove posts with contact details in them.

## Where to start

1. Post "we are open" on your first working day back.
2. Pick the two or three January themes that fit you: Veganuary, Dry January, cold-weather jobs, Self Assessment or a quiet-month offer.
3. Write those posts in one go and schedule them.
4. Set any offer up as a Google offer post with real dates.
5. Keep posting steadily through February, when most competitors go quiet again.
