Semrush Local is a strong local SEO toolkit, and its GBP AI Agent will post to your Google Business Profile, reply to reviews and upload photos for you. For a single UK business the alternatives are Google's free scheduler, a lighter local SEO tool such as Localo, or a posting tool such as postd if what you really want is regular posts on Google and your other pages. Pick Semrush Local when rank tracking and reviews matter as much as posting.

Disclosure first: we make postd. I have tried to give Semrush Local a fair hearing, because for some businesses it is the better tool.

## What is Semrush Local?

Semrush is best known as a website SEO platform used by marketers and agencies. Semrush Local is its toolkit for businesses with a physical location or a service area. You do not need the main Semrush subscription to use it: Semrush says the Local tools [can be added to an existing subscription or bought on their own](https://www.semrush.com/kb/1611-local-toolkit-pricing-and-plans).

The headline feature is the **GBP AI Agent**. According to Semrush's [knowledge base article on the agent](https://www.semrush.com/kb/1605-gbp-ai-agent) (checked December 2026), it:

- optimises your business description
- publishes AI-written posts, **every 7 days by default**, based on the keywords found in its audit of your profile
- replies to new Google reviews **within 24 hours**, in the tone and language you choose
- uploads photos, **every 10 days by default**
- tracks performance for your profile

You can edit a generated post or change its image before it publishes, add instructions for the writing, and choose the call to action button. A "recent actions" list shows what it has done.

## How much does Semrush Local cost?

Semrush Local is priced **per location**. Semrush's [UK pricing page](https://www.semrush.com/pricing/local/) shows pounds:

| Plan | Price per location | What it adds |
|---|---|---|
| Base | £24.31 a month, billed annually | GBP AI Agent, AI post generation and publishing, review auto-replies, automated photo posting, Map Rank Tracker (375 credits) |
| Pro | £48.62 a month, billed annually | Everything in Base, plus listing management across 70+ directories, review management from other sites, review requests by SMS or email, more rank tracking (1,225 credits) |
| Business | Custom quote | For 20 or more locations |

Semrush's knowledge base lists the same plans at $30 and $60 per location a month. The pricing page does not show a monthly-billing price in pounds, so check at checkout if you want to pay month to month. Semrush offers a general seven-day free trial; the pricing page does not say whether it covers Local plans.

For one business with one location, the Base plan works out at about £292 a year.

## What does Semrush Local do well?

- **Rank tracking you can see.** The Map Rank Tracker shows a grid of where you appear in the map results across your area, with competitor heatmaps. For a roofer in Leicester who wants to know why he shows up in Aylestone but not Oadby, that is genuinely useful.
- **Review replies handled.** Replies within 24 hours, every time, is better than most owners manage. You can choose all reviews, positive only or negative only.
- **Listings on Pro.** Pushing your name, address and phone number to 70+ directories, and fixing duplicates, is tedious to do by hand.
- **It fits the rest of Semrush.** If you or your web person already use Semrush for website SEO, everything lives in one place.

## Where does it fall short for a single small business?

- **It is Google only.** The agent article describes Google Business Profile and nothing else. If you want posts on Facebook, LinkedIn or X, you need another tool.
- **Posts come from keywords.** The agent writes posts from the keywords in its audit and your business details. That keeps them on topic for search, but posts built around "emergency plumber Mansfield" can start to read like they are written for Google rather than for customers.
- **Review replies go out on their own.** Semrush's materials describe the replies as automatic. That is convenient, but a reply to an angry one-star review is the one place many owners want to read before it is sent. Pro adds AI reply drafts as part of review management.
- **It is built for marketers.** Credits, grids and audits are great if you like that sort of thing. A café owner who just wants Google kept fresh may find it more than they need.

### Google's free scheduler

Google lets you schedule posts and set them to repeat from inside your Business Profile. If you are happy writing a post a week and replying to your own reviews, this costs nothing. Our [complete guide to Google Business Profile posts](/guides/google-business-profile-posts) shows how.

### Localo

Localo is the closest like-for-like alternative: local SEO help, rank tracking, review replies and auto-generated posts, aimed at owners rather than marketers. It also posts to Facebook.

### postd

postd only does the posting, but it does it differently. It reads your website one page at a time (services, about, FAQ, blog posts) and your real Google reviews, and writes each post from a specific page or a review, rotating the angle so you get service spotlights, tips, FAQs and review quotes rather than the same keyword over and over. When you add a new page to your site, it writes a "what's new" post about it. It publishes to Google Business Profile, Facebook Pages, LinkedIn Company Pages and X, writing a separate version for each, and you can approve every post first or let it run on autopilot. It uses your own photos before any AI image.

It does not reply to reviews, track rankings or manage listings. If you need those, postd is not the answer on its own.

## When is Semrush Local the better pick?

- You want to **see and improve your map rankings**, not just post.
- You get **a lot of Google reviews** and want every one answered quickly.
- Your **listings are a mess** across directories (Pro).
- You or your marketer **already use Semrush**.
- Google is the only platform you care about.

## When is an alternative the better pick?

- **You want Facebook and LinkedIn covered too:** postd or Localo.
- **You want posts that sound like your business, not your keywords:** postd writes from your own pages and reviews. For more on how that stays within the rules, see [turning Google reviews into posts](/guides/turn-google-reviews-into-social-media-posts).
- **You would rather approve every post:** postd's approval queue is on by default.
- **You want local SEO help but simpler:** Localo.
- **Money is tight:** Google's free scheduler, and reply to reviews yourself.

## What does each cost in time and money over a year? A worked example

Take a single-location electrician in Nottingham who wants one Google post a week, gets about 6 Google reviews a month, and would like Facebook covered too.

**Assumptions:** writing and publishing a post by hand takes 15 minutes. Replying to a review takes 3 minutes. Checking a tool-written post takes 2 minutes. Owner time is worth £40 an hour. Tool prices are before VAT, using the annual price where one is offered.

| Setup | What the owner still does each month | Owner time a month | Tool cost a year | Owner time a year, at £40/h | Total a year |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| All by hand (Google scheduler, Facebook by hand) | 4 Google posts, 4 Facebook posts, 6 review replies | 8 x 15 + 6 x 3 = 138 min | £0 | 27.6 h = £1,104 | £1,104 |
| Semrush Local Base | Glance at agent's work (10 min), 4 Facebook posts by hand | 10 + 4 x 15 = 70 min | £292 | 14 h = £560 | £852 |
| postd Local (Google and Facebook), annual | Approve 8 posts, 6 review replies | 8 x 2 + 6 x 3 = 34 min | £150 | 6.8 h = £272 | £422 |

On these assumptions postd comes out cheapest for this electrician, because Facebook is part of what he wants and Semrush Local does not post there. Change the assumptions and the answer changes. If he did not care about Facebook, and wanted rank tracking, Semrush Local Base would leave him only about 10 minutes a month for £292 a year, and that is a reasonable deal.

## Where to start

1. Write down what you want help with: posting, reviews, rankings or listings. Be honest about which you would actually look at.
2. If it is mostly rankings and reviews, and only Google, Semrush Local Base or Localo are the right tools. Try both if you can.
3. If it is mostly regular posts, on Google and the other pages your customers use, try a tool that writes from your own website.
4. If you are on a tight budget, use Google's free scheduler and reply to reviews yourself once a week.
