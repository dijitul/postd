The best Sprout Social alternatives for a small business depend on which half of Sprout you need. For publishing posts, postd writes and schedules posts to Google Business Profile, Facebook, LinkedIn and X from about £15 a month. For the inbox, Postd Office handles Facebook, Instagram and TikTok messages and comments with unlimited seats from £49 a month. Meta Business Suite is free if you only need Facebook and Instagram. ## What is Sprout Social, and who is it for?

Sprout Social is one of the best-known social media management platforms. It does publishing, a shared inbox, reporting, social listening, review management and team workflows, and it is used by in-house marketing teams and agencies. It is well made and well supported. It is also priced for businesses with a marketing budget.

## How much does Sprout Social cost?

Sprout charges **per seat**, so every person who logs in adds to the bill. Prices on Sprout's [pricing page](https://sproutsocial.com/pricing/) are in US dollars (checked December 2026). I have converted at roughly 75p to the dollar, so the pound figures are approximate and before VAT.

| Plan | Price per seat per month | About in pounds | Social profiles | Notable inclusions |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Essentials | $79 billed annually, $99 monthly | about £59, or £74 monthly | 5 | Scheduling, optimal send times, reporting, AI credits |
| Standard | $199 | about £149 | 5 | Adds consolidated inbox, review management, keyword and location monitoring |
| Professional | $299 | about £224 | Unlimited | Adds AI help to improve posts |
| Advanced | $399 | about £299 | Unlimited | Adds things like inbox sentiment and automated rules |
| Enterprise | Custom | | | |

The page sets annual billing as the default and shows only the annual price for Standard and above. There is a 30-day free trial with no card needed. Extra AI credits ("Trellis Plus") cost $28 a seat a month billed annually, and Premium Analytics is a paid add-on priced on request.

The thing to notice is that **the inbox starts on Standard**. If two people in your business answer messages, that is two Standard seats, about £298 a month before VAT.

## What does Sprout Social do well?

Plenty, and it is fair to say so.

- **Reporting.** Sprout's reports are among the best in the category, which matters if you have to show a manager or client what social media is achieving.
- **Breadth of networks.** Its pricing page lists Facebook, Instagram, X, LinkedIn, Threads, Pinterest and TikTok, and its help centre covers [Google Business Profile publishing](https://support.sproutsocial.com/hc/en-us/articles/360041901331-Google-My-Business-Publishing) (What's New and Event posts, not offers or video).
- **Reviews.** Google reviews come into a stream where you can reply, filter and assign them.
- **Workflows.** Approvals, tasks, tagging and, on Advanced, [automated rules](https://support.sproutsocial.com/hc/en-us/articles/360016185371-Automated-Rules) for routing messages.
- **Listening.** Keyword and location monitoring from Standard up.

If you run marketing for a chain of gyms or a regional estate agent, with a team who will use all of this, Sprout earns its price.

## Why do small businesses look for an alternative?

Usually for three reasons:

1. **Per-seat pricing.** A salon where the owner and two front-of-house staff all answer DMs needs three seats.
2. **They only use half of it.** Most local businesses need posts going out and messages answered. They do not need listening, advanced reports or approval chains.
3. **It still does not write the posts.** Sprout has AI help, but someone still has to come up with what to say.

The useful way to replace Sprout is to split it into its two halves: **publishing** and **the inbox**.

## When is Sprout Social the better choice?

- You have a **marketing team** who will use the reports and listening every week.
- You need **one tool for everything**, including Google reviews, LinkedIn messages and approvals across many profiles.
- You report to a board, a franchisor or clients, and **the reports are the product**.
- Budget is not the main concern.

## When is an alternative the better choice?

- You are a **local business with a few people** answering messages and no marketing department.
- You want **posts written for you**, not just scheduled.
- **Instagram and Facebook DMs** are where your bookings and enquiries arrive.
- You would rather not pay **again for every new member of staff**.

## What does it cost? A worked example

Take a restaurant in Nottingham. The owner and two staff answer messages. They want Google, Facebook and LinkedIn posts (for private hire), and an inbox for Facebook and Instagram.

**Assumptions:** Sprout Standard for three seats, annual price. postd Growth and Postd Office Sorting Office, monthly. All prices before VAT; dollars converted at roughly 75p.

| Setup | Monthly cost | Annual cost |
|---|---|---|
| Sprout Social Standard, 3 seats | 3 x $199 = $597, about £448 | about £5,370 |
| postd Growth + Postd Office Sorting Office | £29 + £49 = £78 | £936 (or £780 on annual plans) |
| Meta Business Suite only, posts written by hand | £0 | £0 |

Time matters too. If the owner writes 12 posts a month by hand at 15 minutes each, that is 3 hours, about £120 at £40 an hour. With postd writing them, approving 12 posts at 2 minutes each is 24 minutes. With Sprout, the scheduling is easy, but the writing is still largely the owner's job.

So for this restaurant the split setup saves roughly £370 a month over Sprout, and the owner gives up advanced reports and listening they were unlikely to use. For a marketing team that lives in Sprout's reports, the sum comes out the other way.

## Where to start

1. List what you actually use, or would use, in Sprout: posting, inbox, reviews, reports, listening.

4. If it is mainly the inbox, count the people who answer messages and price per-seat tools against a flat-price one.
