Buffer is one of the best simple schedulers around, and at about £3.80 a channel a month it is hard to beat on price. The main reasons to look elsewhere are that you want the posts written for you, you need someone to approve them, or you want a cheaper way to cover lots of networks. The best alternatives are Publer (cheaper, more networks), Planable (approvals), SocialBee (content recycling), Meta's and Google's free tools, and tools that write posts from your business, such as Localo or postd.

I run dijitul, which makes postd, so bear that in mind. I have tried to say plainly where Buffer, or someone else, is the better choice. Prices were checked on each vendor's pricing page on 20 November 2026 and converted from US dollars at about $1 to £0.76, so pound figures are approximate and exclude VAT.

## What does Buffer do well?

Credit where it is due. Buffer is:

- **Easy.** Most people are scheduling within ten minutes of signing up.
- **Cheap and fair.** [Essentials is $5 per channel a month](https://buffer.com/pricing), about £3.80, and Team is $10 per channel (about £7.60) with unlimited users. Above 10 channels the per-channel price drops.
- **Generous for free.** The free plan covers 3 channels with 10 scheduled posts each.
- **Broad.** It posts to Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, TikTok and more, plus Google Business Profile, where it supports [What's New, Offer and Event posts](https://support.buffer.com/article/557-using-google-business-profiles-with-buffer).
- **Helpful with words.** Its AI Assistant, included on every plan, helps you draft, rewrite and repurpose posts.

If you are a Nottingham salon owner who loves taking photos and writing captions, Buffer is probably all you need.

## Why do people look for a Buffer alternative?

In my experience, the reasons come up in roughly this order:

1. **The scheduler is empty.** Buffer will post anything you give it, but somebody has to think of it and write it. That is the part a busy owner never gets round to.
2. **Google Business Profile is limited.** Buffer handles Google posts, but with one image, no video, no alt text and no Google analytics, per its own help page.
3. **Someone needs to sign posts off.** Buffer has drafts and team access, but not the structured approval steps that agencies or franchises want.
4. **They want an inbox.** Buffer has community engagement features, but teams handling lots of messages often want a dedicated inbox.

### Publer: the closest like-for-like swap

Publer is the most direct Buffer alternative. Its free plan covers 3 accounts (not X), and [paid plans start at $5 a month](https://publer.com/help/en/article/what-are-publers-plans-and-pricing-15h4yqh/) for one account on Professional, or $10 on Business, which adds unlimited AI Assist. Annual billing saves 20%.

On Google it supports updates, events, offers and photo uploads, but [its help centre says](https://publer.com/help/en/article/managing-google-business-profiles-in-publer-rgtjud/) the business must have fewer than 10 locations and video is not supported. It is busier to look at than Buffer, which some people like and some do not.

### SocialBee: for building a library of posts

[SocialBee](https://socialbee.com/pricing/) costs more than Buffer, $29 a month (about £22) for 5 profiles, but it works differently. You sort posts into categories (tips, offers, reviews, team) and it rotates them on a schedule, reusing evergreen posts. Its AI Copilot can draft a strategy and a set of ready-to-use posts. It posts to Google Business Profile on every plan.

### Planable: when someone else signs off

[Planable](https://planable.io/pricing/) charges per workspace, not per user, from $33 a month (about £25) for up to 4 pages and 60 posts a month. Posts appear as they will look, people comment on them directly, and Pro ($49, about £37) lets you make approval required. It is built for agencies and teams, and it is overkill for one person.

### Hootsuite: more, for a lot more

[Hootsuite](https://www.hootsuite.com/plans) starts at $99 per user a month billed annually, about £75, or $149 monthly. You get a full inbox, listening and reporting. Google Business Profile posting is through a [free partner app](https://apps.hootsuite.com/apps/google-my-business). Unless you have a team using it daily, it is a big step up from Buffer's bill.

### The free route: Meta Business Suite plus Google

If you only post to Facebook, Instagram and Google, you can schedule all three for free. [Meta Business Suite](https://www.facebook.com/business/tools/meta-business-suite) handles Facebook and Instagram, and Google Business Profile has [its own scheduling and repeat options](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7342169?hl=en-GB). Our [Google Business Profile posts guide](/guides/google-business-profile-posts) walks through Google's version. The catch is two places to log in and no help with writing.

### Localo: Google first

[Localo](https://localo.com/pricing) is a local SEO tool rather than a scheduler. The Single Business plan, listed at 39 a month on annual billing plus VAT (about £30), autogenerates and publishes Google posts, can publish to Facebook, and tracks your local rankings. If your customers find you on Google Maps and you want the ranking tools too, it is worth a look.

### postd: for when the writing is the problem

postd, which we make, is not a like-for-like Buffer replacement. It does less scheduling and more writing. It reads your website and real Google reviews, writes posts in UK English separately for each platform, and publishes them to Google Business Profile, Facebook Pages, LinkedIn Company Pages and X, either after you approve them or automatically. It uses your own photos first.

What it does not do: Instagram, TikTok, or replying to messages. If Instagram is your main channel, stay with Buffer or move to Publer.

## Buffer vs a tool that writes the posts: a worked example

Let us put numbers on it. These are assumptions, so change them to suit you.

**The business:** a café in Derby posting 3 times a week to Google Business Profile and 3 times a week to Facebook. That is 6 posts a week, about 26 a month.

**Assumptions:**

- Writing a post yourself, including the idea and the photo, takes 10 minutes even with Buffer's AI helping.
- Checking a post a tool has written takes 2 minutes.
- The owner's time is worth £35 an hour.

Buffer is the cheaper tool by about £7 a month. It is not the cheaper way of getting the posts out, unless you were going to spend those four hours writing anyway, in which case it clearly is. If you need Instagram as well, postd does not cover it, so that changes the sums.

## Which Buffer alternative should you pick?

- **Happy with Buffer but want it cheaper or with more networks:** Publer.
- **Want posts reused on a rota:** SocialBee.
- **Need sign-off from a client or partner:** Planable.
- **Running a team inbox:** Hootsuite, or see our [guide to a single social media inbox](/guides/social-media-inbox-small-business).
- **Only Facebook, Instagram and Google, and on a tight budget:** the free tools.
- **The posts are not getting written at all:** a tool that writes them, such as Localo for Google only, or postd for Google, Facebook, LinkedIn and X.
