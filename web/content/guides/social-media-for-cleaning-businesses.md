Cleaning businesses win most new clients from Google searches and local recommendations, so post on your Google Business Profile and a Facebook Page, and add LinkedIn if you want office contracts. The posts that turn one-off enquiries into regular clients are about trust (DBS checks, insurance, the same cleaner every time), clear explanations of what is included, before and after photos and reviews from long-term clients. Below is a 12-month UK calendar, trust posts, examples to copy and a worked example of the time involved.

## Where do people look for a cleaner?

Most people looking for a domestic cleaner search "cleaner near me" or "house cleaning Leicester", or ask in a local Facebook group. Either way, they end up on your Google Business Profile reading reviews. Letting agents and landlords look for end of tenancy cleaners the same way. Offices and commercial clients are more likely to search Google and then check your website and LinkedIn.

| Platform | What it does for a cleaner | Worth it? |
|---|---|---|
| Google Business Profile | Search and Maps, reviews, photos | Yes, always |
| Facebook Page | Local groups and recommendations | Yes |
| LinkedIn Company Page | Offices, letting agents, facilities managers | If you want commercial work |
| Instagram | Satisfying before and after videos | Optional |
| TikTok | Cleaning videos are popular, but viewers are rarely local | Optional |

For the local search side, our guide to [Google Business Profile for tradesmen](/guides/google-business-profile-for-tradesmen) covers the setup that applies to cleaners too.

## What should a cleaning business post?

- **Before and after photos**: ovens, hobs, bathrooms, carpets, windows.
- **What is included** in a regular clean, a deep clean, an end of tenancy clean.
- **Areas you cover** this week.
- **Trust posts**: DBS checks, insurance, how keys are handled, the same cleaner each time.
- **Cleaning tips**: the kind that show you know your stuff, not the kind that replace you.
- **Team spotlights**: who the cleaners are.
- **Reviews**, especially from long-term clients.
- **Availability**: "two regular weekly slots open in Wigston".

## Trust posts for cleaners

Clients are often out when you clean, and they are handing you their keys. Trust is the whole sale.

### DBS checks

Anyone in England or Wales can apply for a [basic DBS check](https://www.gov.uk/request-copy-criminal-record), which shows unspent convictions. Self-employed people can apply for their own, and the government has [guidance for self-employed workers](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/dbs-checks-for-self-employed-people-and-personal-employees). Scotland and Northern Ireland have their own services (Disclosure Scotland and AccessNI). If you and your team have checks, it is the post to make:

> Every cleaner on our team has a current basic DBS check, and we are happy to show you the certificate before your first clean. You are trusting us with your keys and your home, so we think you should be able to see it.

### Insurance

Public liability insurance covers you if you damage something or someone gets hurt. If you employ staff, employers' liability insurance is a legal requirement, with cover of at least £5 million, according to [government guidance](https://www.gov.uk/employers-liability-insurance). Say what you carry, in plain words.

> We carry public liability insurance, so if we ever break or damage something in your home, it is covered. Ask us for a copy of the certificate any time.

Keep the details current, and only claim cover you actually hold.

### The same cleaner every time

For regular clients, continuity matters more than almost anything. If you guarantee the same cleaner, or tell clients in advance when cover is needed, say so.

### Keys and security

How you store keys (coded, never labelled with the address) and what happens if a cleaner leaves. Only describe what you actually do.

### Products

If you use eco-friendly or pet-safe products, or bring your own equipment, say so. Avoid claims like "kills 99.9% of germs" unless the product label says it and you are quoting it accurately.

## Example posts you can copy

**Before and after:**

> Oven clean in Oadby this morning. Racks soaked, glass door taken apart and cleaned between the panes, which is where most of the brown film hides. About two and a half hours from start to finish. Booking ovens before Christmas now.

**What is included:**

> What does a regular weekly clean include? Kitchen surfaces, hob and sink, bathrooms, hoovering and mopping throughout, dusting, beds changed if you leave fresh linen out, and bins emptied. Ovens, fridges and inside windows are extras we can rotate in. Tap Learn more for prices.

**End of tenancy:**

> End of tenancy clean in a two-bed flat in Loughborough. Oven, fridge, skirting boards, inside the cupboards, the bit behind the washing machine. The letting agent signed it off at check-out. Booking June and July end of tenancy cleans for students and landlords now.

**Availability for regulars:**

> We have two regular weekly slots open on Tuesdays in Wigston and Blaby. Same cleaner every week, two or three hours, DBS checked and insured. Tap Book or message us for a quote.

**Seasonal tip:**

> Black mould on your window seals? It is usually condensation. Open the windows for ten minutes each morning, wipe the glass dry, and keep the bathroom fan on after showers. If it has already taken hold, we can deep clean the seals as part of a regular visit.

**Commercial (LinkedIn version):**

> We have just taken on evening cleaning for a 40-person office in Leicester city centre. Same small team every night, keyholding handled, and a monthly deep clean of the kitchen and washrooms. If your office cleaner keeps changing, we would be happy to quote.

## How do you turn reviews into posts?

Reviews from long-term clients are the best thing you can post, because they answer the question every new client has: will they still be good in six months?

1. **Quote word for word**, first name and area only.
2. **Say how long they have been a client**, if they are happy for you to.
3. **Credit the cleaner** if the review names them.
4. **Ask every client**, not just your favourites. Google's [content policy](https://support.google.com/contributionpolicy/answer/7400114?hl=en-GB) bans offering a free clean or discount for reviews, and bans asking only the happy ones.

> "Karen has cleaned for us every Thursday for three years and we still come home and say wow." Thanks, Rob in Market Harborough. Karen says the secret is the same order every week, top to bottom.
