Postd Office is for what comes back: one inbox for Facebook and Instagram messages and comments and TikTok comments, with AI-drafted replies you approve, from £49 a month. If customers find you on Google, start with postd. If they message you on Instagram and Facebook, start with Postd Office. Some businesses need both.

We make both, so this is not a neutral comparison with a rival. It is an honest guide to which of our own products fits you, including where neither does.

## What is postd?

postd reads your website (services, about, FAQ and blog pages) and your real Google reviews, then writes posts in UK English, separately for each platform. It works through your pages so the posts stay specific: a boiler servicing spotlight one week, a quote from a happy customer the next, a tip from your FAQ after that. When a new page appears on your site, it writes a "what's new" post about it.

It publishes at sensible local times, respects quiet hours, uses your own photos first (from your website, your Google profile or uploads) and only makes an AI image when nothing fits. Hashtags are checked against your own words, so you do not get #blessed on a drainage company.

**It does not** post to Instagram or TikTok, and it does not reply to reviews, comments or messages.

## What is Postd Office?

Postd Office puts your Facebook Page messages and comments, Instagram DMs and comments, and TikTok comments in one inbox. TikTok DMs are not included, because TikTok offers no API for them.

Its AI drafts every reply in your business's voice, but **only from answers you have approved**. If it does not know the answer, it leaves the message for a person. Nothing goes out until someone approves it, unless you switch on autopilot for a specific answer you trust, such as "Are you open on Sundays?".

It also translates both ways (the message appears in English, your reply goes back in the customer's language with an English copy), flags abuse, spam, scams and threats, hides comments in a tap, and pushes urgent messages to the top and emails the team. Everyone can have a seat, and nobody needs the Facebook password.

**It does not** publish to Google Business Profile, LinkedIn or X, does not have a LinkedIn or X inbox, and does not handle Google reviews.

## Can you use both together?

Yes. They do different jobs, so they do not overlap much. The one place they meet is **Facebook publishing**: postd posts to Facebook Pages, and Postd Office's Head Office publishes to Facebook too. If you use both, decide which one owns your Facebook posts so customers do not see two posts on the same day.

A sensible split for a business using both:

| Job | Product |
|---|---|
| Google Business Profile posts | postd |
| LinkedIn and X posts | postd |
| Facebook Page posts | One of them, not both |
| Instagram and TikTok posts | Postd Office Head Office |
| Facebook and Instagram DMs and comments, TikTok comments | Postd Office |

They are separate sign-ups with separate billing, so you can start with one and add the other later.

## What does neither product do?

Being clear about this saves you finding out the hard way. Neither postd nor Postd Office:

- **replies to Google reviews.** You still do that in your Business Profile.
- **handles WhatsApp, email, phone calls or website chat.**
- **shows LinkedIn or X messages, or TikTok DMs.**
- **runs or manages paid ads.**
- **tracks your Google Maps rankings** or does local SEO audits. Tools like Localo and Semrush Local do; see [Localo alternatives](/guides/localo-alternative) and [Semrush Local alternatives](/guides/semrush-local-alternative).
- **offers social listening or agency-style reporting.** If you need those, a tool like Sprout Social or Agorapulse is a better fit; see [Sprout Social alternatives](/guides/sprout-social-alternative) and [Agorapulse alternatives](/guides/agorapulse-alternative).
