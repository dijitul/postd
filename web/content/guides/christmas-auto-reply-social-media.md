A Christmas away message is an automatic reply that Facebook and Instagram send to anyone who messages you while you are closed. Set it in Meta Business Suite under Inbox automations, schedule it for your closed dates, and say three things: when you are back, what to do in an emergency, and where to book or buy in the meantime. For 2026, remember the Boxing Day bank holiday falls on Monday 28 December, because 26 December is a Saturday.

This guide gives you the dates, how to set it up, wording you can copy for different trades, and what to check before you switch off.

## What are the UK bank holidays over Christmas 2026?

These are the dates from [GOV.UK's bank holidays page](https://www.gov.uk/bank-holidays), checked in November 2026:

| Date | Day | What it is | Where |
|---|---|---|---|
| 24 December 2026 | Thursday | Christmas Eve (not a bank holiday) | |
| 25 December 2026 | Friday | Christmas Day | England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland |
| 26 December 2026 | Saturday | Boxing Day (weekend, so moved) | |
| 28 December 2026 | Monday | Boxing Day, substitute bank holiday | England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland |
| 31 December 2026 | Thursday | New Year's Eve (not a bank holiday) | |
| 1 January 2027 | Friday | New Year's Day | England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland |
| 4 January 2027 | Monday | 2 January, substitute bank holiday | Scotland only |

The Monday 28 December substitute day catches people out. Customers will message on that Monday expecting you to be open, and some suppliers will be closed when you are not. Put it in your away message explicitly.

## Instant reply or away message: which one do you need?

Meta Business Suite has several automated replies. [Meta's help centre on automated responses](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/395965998733706) lists them; the two that matter at Christmas are:

| | Instant reply | Away message |
|---|---|---|
| When it sends | The first time someone messages you | Only during times you have marked yourself as away |
| Best for | A year-round "thanks, we will reply within X" | Closures, holidays, evenings |
| Christmas use | Change the wording to mention your Christmas hours | Schedule it for the days you are shut |

Meta's help centre also says that if a keyword reply and an away message are both triggered, the away message is the one that goes. So during your closure, the away message wins.

## How do you set a Christmas away message on Facebook and Instagram?

Meta moves its menus around, so check [Meta's guide to Inbox automations](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/318238182723007) if a button has moved. On desktop, the steps are broadly:

1. Open **Meta Business Suite** and go to **Inbox**.
2. Select **Automations** (sometimes shown as automated responses).
3. Choose **Away message**.
4. Pick the channels: Messenger, Instagram or both. Instagram needs your professional account connected to the Page.
5. Set the schedule for the times you are closed. Add each block of days separately if you are open in between.
6. Write the message (see the templates below). Keep it short, because people read it on a phone.
7. Save, then **test it**: send your Page a message from a personal account during a time the schedule says you are away.

Do the same for your instant reply if you have one: update it to mention the Christmas dates, then change it back in January.

## What should a Christmas away message say?

Three things, in this order:

1. **When you are back**, with a day and a date. "Back Tuesday 29 December" beats "back after the holidays".
2. **What to do if it cannot wait.** An emergency number with its hours, or an honest "we are not taking emergency call-outs".
3. **What they can do now.** A booking link, gift voucher link, menu or price list.

Things to leave out: long festive poems, every opening time for the next fortnight (link to them instead), and anything you will not stick to.

### Templates you can copy

**Plumber or heating engineer (with emergency cover), Mansfield:**

> Thanks for your message. We are closed from Thursday 24 December and back on Tuesday 29 December. If you have no heating or a leak you cannot stop, call our emergency line, staffed 8am to 8pm every day except Christmas Day. For anything else, leave your postcode and what is wrong and we will reply when we are back. Merry Christmas.

**Hair or beauty salon, Nottingham:**

> Hi, thanks for getting in touch. The salon is closed from 25 to 28 December and open again on Tuesday 29 December. You can book any January appointment now using the link on our profile, and e-gift vouchers are on there too. We will answer messages when we are back.

**Café, Derby:**

> Thanks for your message. We are closed on Christmas Day and Boxing Day, then open as normal from Sunday 27 December, apart from New Year's Day. For table bookings over the holidays, use the booking link on our page. We read messages once a day while we are closed.

**Cleaning business, Leicester:**

> Thanks for your message. We are taking a break from 24 December to 3 January and back on Monday 4 January. Regular clients, your January slots are already in the diary. New enquiries, send your postcode and the size of the property and we will get a quote to you in the first week back.

**Scottish business, two weeks off:**

> Thanks for your message. We are closed from Thursday 24 December and back on Tuesday 5 January, after the Scottish bank holiday on Monday 4 January. Leave your name and what you need and we will reply in order when we are back.

Change the dates to yours. If you are open some days in the gap, say which, rather than giving a single "back on" date that is wrong for half the week.

## Will you still be able to reply when you get back?

In Meta's own apps (Facebook, Instagram, Meta Business Suite) you can usually pick conversations up when you are back.

If you use a third-party inbox tool, there is a rule worth knowing. Tools that connect through Meta's messaging systems follow the [Messenger Platform and Instagram Messaging API policy](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/messenger-platform/policy). For most messages, that gives a 24-hour window to reply. A person replying by hand can use a Human Agent tag, which gives a 7-day window. So:

- **A short closure is fine.** A message on 24 December answered by a person on 29 December is inside 7 days.
- **A long closure can leave early messages outside the window.** Someone who messaged on 22 December and gets a reply on 5 January is past 7 days. For those, reply in Meta's own app, or answer the urgent ones before you go.

Postd Office connects through Meta's API, so the same windows apply to it.

Before you close:

1. Decide your closed dates, and check them against Monday 28 December and Friday 1 January (and Monday 4 January in Scotland).

6. Answer anything outstanding from the week before you close.
