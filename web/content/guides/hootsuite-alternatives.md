Hootsuite is a capable platform, but at $99 per user a month on annual billing (about £75), or $149 month to month, it is priced for marketing teams. Most UK small businesses use it for two jobs: getting posts out, and replying to comments and messages. You can usually do both for less by splitting them: a cheap scheduler such as Buffer or Publer, or a tool that writes the posts such as postd, plus a dedicated inbox such as Meta Business Suite (free) or Postd Office.

Disclosure: I run dijitul, which makes both postd and Postd Office. I will say where Hootsuite, or another tool, is the better pick. Prices were checked on each vendor's own pricing page in November 2026. Dollar prices are converted at about $1 to £0.76, so pound figures are approximate and before VAT.

## What does Hootsuite cost in 2026?

From [Hootsuite's plans page](https://www.hootsuite.com/plans):

| Plan | Billed annually | Billed monthly | Social accounts | Inbox |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Standard | $99 per user a month (about £75) | $149 per user a month (about £113) | 10 | Shared inbox, saved replies |
| Professional | $199 per user a month (about £151) | $299 per user a month (about £227) | Unlimited | Adds DM automations and AI smart replies |
| Advanced | $399 per user a month (about £303) | $499 per user a month (about £379) | Unlimited | Adds assigning messages to teammates |
| Enterprise | Quote | Quote | Unlimited | Advanced inbox |

Every paid plan includes Hootsuite's AI tools for content and hashtag suggestions, with 100 AI credits a month, and there is a 14-day free trial with daily posting limits.

Note the "per user". A Leicester cleaning company with an owner and an office manager both logging in pays for two seats.

## What is Hootsuite genuinely good at?

- **Everything in one place.** Publishing, inbox, social listening, analytics and reporting.
- **Teams.** On Advanced, assigning messages to the right person with routing and productivity reports.
- **Scale.** Unlimited accounts from Professional up.
- **Reviews.** Since [April 2026](https://www.hootsuite.com/whats-new/google-business-profile-reviews-in-inbox), Google Business Profile reviews show up in the Inbox, filterable by star rating and location.

If you are a multi-site business with a marketing person, monthly reports for a board, and a steady flow of messages, Hootsuite (or Sprout Social) is a sensible tool and the cheaper options below will feel thin.

## Why do small businesses leave Hootsuite?

- **The price per user.** Two people on Standard is about £150 a month billed annually.
- **Paying for features they do not use.** Listening and advanced reporting are wasted on a café.
- **Google is not front and centre.** Hootsuite does not list Google Business Profile as a network on its plans page; posting goes through a [free partner app made by AOT](https://apps.hootsuite.com/apps/google-my-business).
- **The posts still need writing.** The AI tools help, but they do not decide what to say each week.

**Buffer** is the obvious step down: simple, cheap and broad. Its Team plan, at $10 per channel, gives unlimited users, which suits a small team far better than Hootsuite's per-seat pricing. More in [Buffer alternatives](/guides/buffer-alternatives).

**Publer** is the budget pick, with a free plan for 3 accounts and Google post types including offers and events.

**Planable** keeps the approvals side of Hootsuite at a per-workspace price, which is what agencies tend to miss most when they leave.

**Sprout Social** is not cheaper per seat at the higher tiers, but its Essentials plan, at $79 per seat a month on annual billing, is a lower entry point for teams that want an enterprise-style tool.

## Hootsuite vs splitting the job: a worked example

Assumptions, so you can change them:

- **The business:** a cleaning company in Leicester with two people who handle social media (owner and office manager).
- **Posting:** 3 posts a week each on Google Business Profile, Facebook and LinkedIn, so about 39 posts a month.
- **Messages:** a steady trickle of Facebook and Instagram enquiries.
- **Writing time:** 10 minutes per post if you write it yourself; 2 minutes to check a post a tool wrote.
- **Time is worth** £35 an hour.

| | Hootsuite Standard, 2 users, annual | Buffer Team (3 channels) plus Meta Business Suite | postd Growth plus Postd Office Sorting Office |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tool cost a month | 2 x $99 = $198, about £150 | 3 x $10 = $30, about £23 | £29 + £49 = £78 plus VAT |
| Writing time a month | 39 x 10 min = 6.5 hours | 6.5 hours | 39 x 2 min = 1.3 hours |
| Value of writing time at £35/hour | about £228 | about £228 | about £46 |
| Total | about £378 | about £251 | about £124 plus VAT |

(The time spent answering messages is left out, because it depends entirely on how many you get. Postd Office drafts replies, which shortens it; Meta Business Suite does not.)

The cheapest tool bill is Buffer plus Meta's free inbox, and if your team is happy writing the posts, that is the sensible answer. Hootsuite only wins if you use what the others do not have: listening, detailed reporting or assigning messages across a bigger team.

## Which Hootsuite alternative is right for you?

- **You want Hootsuite, just cheaper:** Buffer Team, or Publer.
- **Approvals with clients matter most:** Planable.
- **You only need Facebook and Instagram replies:** Meta Business Suite.
- **You want replies drafted for you across Facebook, Instagram and TikTok:** Postd Office.
- **You want the posts written:** postd for Google, Facebook, LinkedIn and X.
- **You genuinely use listening and reports:** stay on Hootsuite, or compare Sprout Social.
