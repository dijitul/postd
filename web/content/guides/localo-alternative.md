The main alternatives to Localo for Google Business Profile posting are Google's own free scheduler, a writing-and-posting tool such as postd, a bigger local SEO suite such as Semrush Local, or a general scheduler like Buffer. Localo is a local SEO tool with posting built in. If you mostly want the posts written and published, on Google and your other pages, postd does that for less. If you want rank tracking and step-by-step help with your profile, Localo is the better pick.

We make postd, so read this with that in mind. I have tried to be straight about where Localo is the stronger product.

## What does Localo actually do?

Localo is a Google Business Profile tool aimed at owners who want more calls from Google Maps without learning local SEO. According to its [pricing page](https://localo.com/pricing) (checked November 2026), the Single Business plan covers one Business Profile and includes:

- autogenerated posts published to your profile, and posts and offers shared on Google and Facebook
- a position map and keyword tracking, so you can see where you appear in the map results around you
- competitor views, so you can see what nearby rivals do
- guided tasks meant to improve your rankings, with an AI agent and regular profile audits
- reviews collected in one place, with one-click replies

That is a lot more than posting. Localo is really trying to be your local SEO helper, and posts are one part of it.

## What does postd do differently?

postd is narrower. It does one job: it writes your posts and publishes them. It does not track rankings, audit your profile or reply to reviews.

What it does instead:

- **It reads your own website and your real Google reviews**, then writes posts from them: a service spotlight from your boiler servicing page, a tip from your FAQ, a review quoted word for word. When a new page appears on your site, it writes a "what's new" post about it.
- **It writes separately for each platform.** Google Business Profile, Facebook Pages, LinkedIn Company Pages and X each get their own version, not one post pasted four times.
- **It uses your own photos first**, from your website, your Google profile or ones you upload, and only makes an AI image when none fits.
- **You choose how much control you keep.** Approve each post in a queue, edit anything, or switch on autopilot.

It does not post to Instagram or TikTok, and it does not reply to reviews or messages.

## What are the other Localo alternatives?

### Google's own scheduler (free)

Since late 2025 you can schedule posts and set them to repeat from inside your Business Profile, at no cost. Our [complete guide to Google Business Profile posts](/guides/google-business-profile-posts) explains how. It is the right answer if you are happy to write every post yourself and only care about Google. It does not write anything, and it does not touch Facebook or LinkedIn.

### Semrush Local

Semrush Local is the heavyweight option. Its UK pricing page shows the Base plan at [£24.31 per location a month, billed annually](https://www.semrush.com/pricing/local/), with a GBP AI Agent that, according to Semrush's [knowledge base](https://www.semrush.com/kb/1611-local-toolkit-pricing-and-plans), publishes posts on a schedule, replies to Google reviews and posts photos, plus map rank tracking. The Pro plan adds listings and review management. It suits someone who already uses Semrush for their website SEO, or an agency.

### A general scheduler

Tools like Buffer and Hootsuite schedule posts you have written to lots of platforms. They do not know anything about your business, so the writing is still yours. If that is what you are after, see [Buffer alternatives for UK small businesses](/guides/buffer-alternatives) and [cheaper Hootsuite alternatives](/guides/hootsuite-alternatives).

## When is Localo the better choice?

Pick Localo if:

- **You want to rank higher in the map results**, and you want someone (or something) to tell you what to fix. Rank tracking, competitor views and guided tasks are exactly what postd does not offer.
- **You get a lot of Google reviews** and want to reply to them quickly from one place.
- **Google and Facebook are the only places you care about.** If you are never going to post on LinkedIn or X, postd's wider platform list is not worth anything to you.

Be aware that posts alone do not move rankings much. Google's own [explanation of local ranking](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7091?hl=en-GB) talks about relevance, distance and prominence and does not mention posts. If ranking is the goal, the SEO side of Localo is what you are paying for.

## When is postd the better choice?

Pick postd if:

- **You want the posts written from what your business actually does.** postd works page by page through your website and quotes your real reviews, so a salon in Nottingham gets posts about its balayage and its brow lamination, not generic "treat yourself" filler.
- **You also want Facebook, LinkedIn or X covered.** An accountant in Derby might care more about LinkedIn than Google. A café might want X for daily specials.
- **You want to see each post before it goes out**, at least for the first few weeks.
- **Budget matters.** £15 a month plus VAT on the Local plan covers Google and one other platform.
